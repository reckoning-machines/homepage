What is reuse worth to your team?

Choose a question your customers ask every quarter. Start with the last answer your analysts signed off on, the disclosure behind it and the next filing. Those give your product and research teams a concrete test of what your platform could carry forward.

Compare two research cycles

Can an analyst trust the answer?
Check the figure, the governing passage and the conditions on its use. When the next filing changes the definition, check whether the platform identifies the decision that needs review.
What work carries forward?
Have another analyst take the next question. Record which decisions they can reuse, which sources they must revisit and which new judgments they need to make.
Does reuse pay for the work it takes?
Measure analyst time for setup, approval, corrections and maintenance alongside model and integration costs. Compare the total with your current workflow at your actual mix of new and recurring questions.

What the cost study measured

Across 44 requests, Context Compiler saved $0.88 in measured model inference expense. The study allocated $2.12 to preparation alone. The value of analyst time saved remains an open question for a customer evaluation.
