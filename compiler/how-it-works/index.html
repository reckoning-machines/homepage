How it works

A researcher finds a difference that matters, such as a change in how a bank reports a deposit rate. The team decides what the filing supports. Context Compiler keeps that decision with its evidence so the next researcher can use it within the same limits.

From research to reusable knowledge

Acquisition
A research agent proposes an interpretation for a specific use and identifies the passages that support it.
Governance
Your review system records who approved that interpretation, when it was approved and who may use it.
Accumulation
Each approved decision and its evidence survive the research session in a workspace your application controls.
Reasoning
For a new question, the compiler supplies the applicable knowledge and evidence. A missing definition gives the team its next research task.

Where Context Compiler fits

RAG makes information accessible. Knowledge engineering makes analytical meaning explicit.

Context Compiler builds your knowledge graph as you research. Start with one question. Each definition your team approves becomes part of the graph, linked to the evidence behind it.

Design objective · Knowledge-graph-quality reasoning without knowledge-graph-scale engineering.

Developer integration

Context Compiler is a small, standalone Python library that runs inside your research application. Connect your existing document catalog, evidence reader and authorization infrastructure. Your application supplies a private workspace directory and retains control of approvals and access.
