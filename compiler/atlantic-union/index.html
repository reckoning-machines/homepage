What the integration does

The host imports ResearchWorkspace from context_compiler_host and supplies a filing catalog, an evidence reader, an authenticated approval lookup and a private workspace directory.

Build the knowledge you need today. propose() binds the claim, filing, permitted use and evidence to a review card. admit() checks the host’s decision for that exact card, reopens the evidence and saves the approved material.

Reuse it tomorrow. A later process uses derive() to rebuild native assertions and evidence links from the saved material. compile() resolves the approved knowledge for the requested filing, use, access scope and time.

Let it grow as the research expands. A reusable investment host profile supplies the typed declarations for reviewed interpretations, monetary amounts and source excerpts. Each new admission adds filing-specific knowledge under those same declarations.

What the demonstration establishes

Historical import. The replay used two recorded proposals and their actual human approvals. We manually assigned two permitted uses, bound the cards to their filing versions and evidence locations, and mapped one recorded operand to the March amount. The reviewer had approved the amount, unit and meaning in prose. The machine-readable binding came later. Professional labor was unmetered, so total knowledge-engineering savings remain unmeasured.

Prospective acquisition. A separate deterministic test proposed a previously unseen permitted use and monetary value under the same domain pack. Admission required a test reviewer’s approval. A fresh workspace then compiled the new use and resolved the value. This establishes the acquisition-to-compilation handoff. Autonomous discovery and unrestricted generation of financial formulas remain outside the demonstrated capability.
