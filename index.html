Advisory

Two kinds of client.

An engagement begins with the process a team already runs and the decisions it has to defend, and ends with a system the team can operate without us.

Investment companies

For a research or portfolio team, an engagement addresses how frontier models enter the research process without the firm losing the record of what was proposed, what evidence it rested on and who decided, so that an estimate can be explained to a PM or an investment committee months after it was made.

Corporate FP&A teams

For a planning team, an engagement addresses the forecast model and the process around it: which assumptions are judgment and which are formula, how a model can draft an assumption from the evidence, and how the team approves and records that draft, so that the plan the board sees carries its reasoning.

Point of view

What frontier models change.

A frontier model can now read a filing, draft a forecast assumption and argue for it in a form a careful analyst would recognize, which means the scarce step in research is no longer producing the proposal but deciding what to do with it. The teams that benefit will be the ones whose process keeps what the model proposed, the evidence it relied on and the decision a person made about it, because a conclusion that cannot be traced back to its proposal is worth less as the model that produced it gets better.

AI that proposes, a record of what it proposed, and a person who decides.

Built by Reckoning Machines

Two systems that hold to that view.

IRIS

An IRIS model is a company forecast in Excel, enriched with the reasoning behind each key driver, written down as a Method the analyst reviews and approves, and with the research and judgments the analyst chooses to keep, each bound to the exact workbook state it was made in. IRIS is the catalog of those models, prepared company by company with their coverage and checks stated, and it is searchable across a portfolio, so a PM can put one question to the whole book and have it answered company by company from the models directly. The analyst works in Excel with Claude alongside, and with Forecast an approved Method runs against new evidence with its inputs, reasoning and results kept together.

Supervised pilot. Access is arranged with us.

Adaptive Research Harness

Alpha decays, and so do the methods that find it. Your research agents already generate more ideas than your team can evaluate, and every round of testing and revision makes it harder to tell a result that will hold up from the best-looking outcome of many attempts. Coding agents work inside harnesses that manage execution, preserve state and check their work, and the Adaptive Research Harness brings that discipline to a research swarm. Every attempt is recorded with its cost and its failures, a separate check stands between a result and the accepted record, and the history tells you what to test next and what to retire. When a strategy is challenged, you can show how it was selected.

Open prototype on GitHub. Runs on synthetic data from two commands.
