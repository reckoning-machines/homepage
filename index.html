Advisory

The work starts inside the client's own forecast.

An engagement begins with the process a team already runs and the decisions it has to defend, and ends with a system the team can operate without us.

Investment companies

A research team wants frontier models on its filings and workbooks without surrendering the estimate. We work out where a model belongs in the process and what the PM sees when a number moves. The analyst stays responsible for the forecast.

Corporate FP&A teams

Most planning models live in one workbook that a few people fully understand. An engagement maps which assumptions are judgment and which are formula, then sets the model up so a frontier model can refresh the formula side from the evidence. The board gets a plan whose assumptions can be traced.

Point of view

Cheap models move the value to the process around them.

A frontier model can read a 10-K and draft a forecast assumption at almost no cost, so the cost of building a model has collapsed. Analysis that used to be an edge is becoming a commodity, and the advantage moves to the process around the model. Teams that keep their forecasts in one place, with the assumptions written down and testable, will get more out of the same models than teams that paste answers into a spreadsheet.

AI that proposes, a record of what it proposed, and a person who decides.

Built by Reckoning Machines

Two systems built on the same rule.

IRIS

An IRIS model is a company forecast in Excel that carries the reasoning behind each key driver, written down as a Method the analyst reviews and approves, together with the research the analyst chooses to keep, bound to the exact workbook state it was made in. IRIS is the catalog of those models, prepared company by company with their coverage and checks stated, and searchable across a portfolio, so a PM can put one question to the whole book and have it answered company by company from the models directly. The analyst works in Excel with Claude alongside.

Supervised pilot. Access is arranged with us.

Adaptive Research Harness

Alpha decays, and so do the methods that find it, and now that research agents generate more ideas than a team can evaluate, it is harder to tell a result that will hold up from the best-looking outcome of many attempts. Coding agents work inside harnesses that manage execution, preserve state and check their work, and the Adaptive Research Harness brings that discipline to a research swarm, recording every attempt with its cost and its failures and putting a separate check between a result and the accepted record. When a strategy is challenged, the history shows how it was selected.

Open prototype on GitHub. Runs on synthetic data from two commands.
