IRIS

Ask the whole book.

IRIS turns your financial models into a research system. Trace an earnings estimate to the assumptions behind it, then see where those assumptions matter across your portfolio.

  • Start with an executable financial model from the IRIS Model Catalog.
  • Consult the Factory’s accumulated research methods and the evidence behind them, even before adopting a method for your forecast.
  • Keep your own research, assumptions and reasoning as the thesis evolves.
  • Ask Claude what drives earnings at one company or where the same exposure appears across your portfolio.
  • Run deterministic earnings sensitivities using the model’s formulas and the assumptions you choose.
  • Keep setting your own earnings estimates in Excel. IRIS works even if you never use AI to forecast a single cell.

IRIS gives Claude a compact account of each model through semantic compression. You decide whether to run a proposed change.

Supervised pilot with two investment firms. Access is arranged with us.

Adaptive Research Harness | DIALOG

The Adaptive Research Harness holds research agents to account for what they try, and Dialog is the trading desk that runs on it.

  • Research agents now produce more ideas than a team can check, and the best-looking result of many attempts is not the same as one that will hold up.
  • ARH writes each attempt to the record before the next can start, with its question, its reserved budget, its result and its failures, so the count of attempts is a fact of the record rather than a recollection.
  • A separate evaluator checks each proposed conclusion, and a result enters the accepted record only under rules the owner has set.
  • Rejected work stays in the record, so the next researcher sees the failed variants and the decisions behind what was accepted.
  • Researchers and evaluators can be replaced while the acceptance rules stay in place, so a team can change its agents without losing what it has learned.
  • Dialog puts the harness to work on a paper desk. Competing research approaches develop hypotheses, an independent evaluator checks them, and the strategies that pass share one portfolio with orders, fills, cash and fees accounted for.

Budget makes the search visible and separate acceptance makes the result accountable, so when a strategy is challenged the history shows how much searching it took to find it and why it was accepted.

Open prototypes on GitHub. Synthetic data and paper execution.

For a global payments company we built the profitability and unit-economics model for a new product and paired it with Monte Carlo scenario analysis. A large pharmaceutical company needed a way to decide which AI-enabled commercial investments to fund and which to test first. We built that decision framework on Monte Carlo and value-of-information analysis.

Point of view

As code gets cheaper, context and control matter more.

AI makes it easier to build and run forecasts, tests, and research agents. At scale, teams need to know which assumptions and evidence produced each result, what changed, and why a conclusion was accepted.

AI that proposes, a record of what it proposed, and a person who decides.
